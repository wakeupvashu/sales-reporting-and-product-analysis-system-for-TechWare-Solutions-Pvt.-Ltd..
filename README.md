# sales-reporting-and-product-analysis-system-for-TechWare-Solutions-Pvt.-Ltd..
This project is essentially a sales reporting and product analysis system for TechWare Solutions Pvt. Ltd.. The uploaded Excel file represents a Monthly Sales Report for January 2024, and it is structured to capture both transaction-level details and product-level performance summaries.
Key Components of the Project
Sales Transactions Sheet

Records each transaction with:

Transaction ID

Date

Product Name

Quantity sold

Unit Price

Discount %

Total Amount

Example:
On 21-01-2024, a Desktop Workstation was sold at ₹120,000 with a 10% discount.

Summary Report

Provides overall business metrics:

Total Transactions

Total Revenue

Total Units Sold

Average Order Value

Highest Sale

Lowest Sale

This section helps management quickly assess monthly performance.

Product Analysis Sheet

Aggregates sales data by product:

Units Sold

Total Sales

Percentage contribution to overall revenue

Example:
Laptop Pro 15, Desktop Workstation, Monitor 4K, etc., are compared to see which product drives the most revenue.

🔹 Purpose of the Project
To track and monitor sales performance on a monthly basis.

To identify top-selling products and those with lower demand.

To analyze discounts and pricing strategies and their impact on revenue.

To provide decision-making insights for inventory management, marketing, and sales strategy.
